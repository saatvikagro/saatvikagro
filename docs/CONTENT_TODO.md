### Products (7 Total — Shakti Range)

**1. Shakti Maize Starch Powder Food Grade**
Shakti Maize Starch Powder is a premium food-grade starch engineered for consistency, stability, and superior functional performance across diverse food and industrial applications. Derived from carefully selected maize and refined through precision processing, it delivers reliable texture, clarity, and structure batch after batch.

**2. Shakti Maize Starch Powder For Industrial Use**
Shakti Maize Starch Powder – Industrial Use is a high-performance starch engineered to deliver consistent binding, viscosity, and stability across demanding industrial applications. Manufactured under controlled processes, it ensures dependable performance, process efficiency, and uniform results in textiles, paper, adhesives, and allied industries.

**3. Shakti Liquid Glucose**
Shakti Liquid Glucose is a high-purity, food-grade glucose syrup engineered for consistent sweetness, smooth texture, and dependable performance across confectionery, dairy, bakery, and beverage applications. Produced through controlled processing and stringent quality systems, it delivers clarity, stability, and moisture balance batch after batch, supporting efficient and scalable food manufacturing.

**4. Shakti Maltodex LD, SD, HD**
Shakti Maltodex is a versatile maltodextrin ingredient family engineered through controlled hydrolysis to deliver consistent purity, functionality, and performance across food and nutrition applications. Available in Low, Standard, and High dextrose equivalent profiles, it allows formulators to precisely balance body, solubility, sweetness control, and energy release without compromising on quality.

**5. Shakti Gluten Meal**
Shakti Gluten Meal is a high-protein, low-fat maize-based feed ingredient developed to deliver balanced nutrition, digestibility, and consistent performance in poultry, cattle, and aquaculture feed formulations. Produced through a refined separation process, it offers a clean, golden appearance, stable nutrient profile, and reliable amino acid and fibre content.

**6. Shakti Maize Dry Fibre**
Shakti Maize Dry Fibre is a clean, fibre-rich maize ingredient developed to deliver steady energy, digestive support, and consistent nutritional performance in cattle and poultry feed formulations. Produced through a careful separation process that preserves the natural structure and digestibility of maize fibre.

**7. Shakti Maize Germ**
Shakti Maize Germ is a nutrient-dense, oil-rich maize ingredient developed to deliver sustained energy, essential nutrition, and dependable performance across animal nutrition and industrial applications. Naturally high in oil, amino acids, and vital nutrients.
