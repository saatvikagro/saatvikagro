At Saatvik Agro Processors Private Limited (“Saatvik Agro”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), we take your privacy and corporate confidentiality with utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard business and personal information gathered across our website, commercial contact portals, and customer correspondence.

1. Overview & Scope

This policy applies to information collected through our official website www.saatvikagro.com, procurement inquiries, product brochure downloads, commercial quotation forms, and sample dispatch requests.

Saatvik Agro complies with relevant data protection legislation including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

2. Information We Collect

As an agro-ingredient manufacturer serving B2B enterprises, pharmaceutical formulators, food processors, and animal feed producers, the information we collect primarily relates to commercial and professional transactions:

  • Contact Identifiers: Full name, official corporate email address, business telephone/mobile number, and job title/designation.
  • Company & Procurement Details: Registered company name, corporate headquarters address, GST/tax identification number, factory delivery location, and target application industry.
  • Product Interest & Sample Data: Selected product requirements (e.g., Food Grade Starch, Liquid Glucose, Maltodextrin, Animal Nutrition), target monthly volumes, sample delivery consignee address, and technical specification requests.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP address, browser type and version, device operating system, referring URL, time spent per page, and general geolocation (city/country level).

3. How We Collect Data

We collect information through transparent, voluntary channels:

  • Direct Website Forms: When you submit our commercial inquiry form on Contact Us.
  • Sample & Brochure Portals: When you download product technical sheets, Certificates of Analysis (COA), or request formulation samples on Products.
  • Direct Commercial Communications: When you call our commercial helpline, email our marketing desk, or initiate a chat via our verified WhatsApp business channel.
  • Automated Server Logs: Standard web analytics and security logs collected as you navigate our web pages.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

The information collected is used strictly for legitimate commercial and business operations:

Data Category Primary Business Purpose
Contact & Corporate Info Responding to RFQs, sending formal price quotations, and client relationship management.
Delivery & Sample Info Dispatching certified laboratory samples via courier and issuing test batch documentation.
Technical Specification Data Ensuring plant production batches align with specific client viscosity, DE, and moisture criteria.
Website Analytics Enhancing page load speeds, maintaining cybersecurity, and optimizing user navigation.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal and corporate information on one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: When you voluntarily submit forms, request sample dispatches, or opt in to receive updates.
  • Contractual Performance: To fulfill pre-contractual commercial inquiries, supply proforma invoices, and manage logistics dispatches.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: To satisfy statutory accounting, GST reporting, food safety audits (FSSAI), and tax regulations.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our network security, prevent fraudulent quotation requests, and enhance B2B services.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes essential and performance cookies to provide a smooth user experience.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site navigation, form submission security, and CSRF protection.
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymized metrics helping our development team monitor site health, traffic volume, and popular product categories.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may limit the responsiveness of interactive contact forms.

7. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

Strict No-Sale Policy

Saatvik Agro Processors Private Limited does NOT sell, lease, rent, or trade your corporate or personal data to marketing brokers or third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

Information may only be shared with trusted third parties under strict confidentiality agreements for essential operational workflows:

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Third-party express couriers (e.g., Blue Dart, DTDC, DHL) solely for delivering physical product samples and test materials to your facility.
  • Cloud & IT Service Providers: Trusted enterprise hosting and communication platforms maintaining industry-standard encryption.
  • Regulatory & Legal Authorities: Government bodies, FSSAI officials, or judicial courts only when mandatory under applicable Indian law.

8. Data Security & Storage

We employ robust physical, administrative, and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure of your information.

  • All website communication is protected via 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS) encryption.
  • Access to commercial lead databases and customer correspondence is restricted to authorized marketing and procurement executives on a need-to-know basis.
  • Data is hosted on secure, regularly audited enterprise servers with active firewall monitoring.

9. Your Data Rights & Choices

Under applicable Indian data protection frameworks, you have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal information we hold concerning your inquiries.
  • Right to Correction: You may request rectification of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated business records.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your contact data from our marketing mailing lists, subject to statutory retention obligations.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke consent for commercial communications at any time by contacting our privacy desk.

10. Data Retention Period

We retain personal and commercial inquiry records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the business purposes for which they were collected, including satisfying commercial warranty, audit, tax, or legal reporting obligations. When no longer required, data is securely erased or anonymized.
