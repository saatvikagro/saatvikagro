    About Us

    From grain to greatness, we engineer consistency you can trust.

    Saatvik Agro is the agro-ingredient unit of the Saatvik Group, dedicated to creating high quality maize-based ingredients that power everyday products across food, nutrition, animal feed, and industrial applications.

    Rooted in purity and strengthened by science, we convert responsibly sourced maize into functional, reliable ingredients that meet the evolving needs of modern manufacturers. Every product we make is guided by a single belief: better ingredients build better outcomes.

    Values

    Strong Values. Strong Ingredients.

    At Saatvik Agro, our values are built on the belief that ingredients matter. What goes into food, feed, and industry must be pure, safe, and responsibly produced. We combine ethical sourcing, disciplined processing, and continuous improvement to ensure that every ingredient reflects integrity and care. Our purpose goes beyond manufacturing. We exist to support healthier products, stronger supply chains, and a better future.

    Manufacturing

    Manufactured with Precision. Delivered with Confidence.

    Our manufacturing operations are designed for consistency, hygiene, and efficiency. From maize selection to final dispatch, every stage is monitored to maintain strict quality benchmarks. Advanced process controls and quality labs ensure uniformity in texture, moisture, granulation, and performance, enabling manufacturers to operate with confidence and predictability.

    • Fully automated manufacturing unit
    • Dedicated quality assurance systems
    • Scalable production capacity
