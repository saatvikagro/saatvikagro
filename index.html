What We Offer

WHY CHOOSE SAATVIK AGRO

Nourishing industries with
pure ingredients

Saatvik Agro is the agro-ingredient unit of the Saatvik Group, dedicated to creating high quality maize-based ingredients that power everyday products across food, nutrition, animal feed, and industrial applications.

Rooted in purity and strengthened by science, we convert responsibly sourced maize into functional, reliable ingredients that meet the evolving needs.

Food and Beverages

Texture, body and thickening for food and beverage formulations

Confectionery and Bakery

Sweetness, texture and moisture retention
