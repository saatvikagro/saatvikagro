    Food & Nutrition

    Shakti Maltodex LD, SD, HD

    Shakti Maltodex is a versatile maltodextrin ingredient family engineered through controlled hydrolysis to deliver consistent purity, functionality, and performance across food and nutrition applications. Available in Low, Standard, and High dextrose equivalent profiles, it allows formulators to precisely balance body, solubility, sweetness control, and energy release without compromising on quality.

    With a clean, neutral flavour, excellent dispersibility, and predictable batch-to-batch behaviour, Shakti Maltodex supports applications ranging from beverages and bakery to dairy, confectionery, and specialised nutrition, offering functional choice through science-led precision rather than quality differentiation.
