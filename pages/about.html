    About Saatvik Agro

    From grain to greatness, we engineer consistency you can trust.

    Our Story

    Rooted in Purity, Strengthened by Science.

    Saatvik Agro is the agro-ingredient unit of the Saatvik Group, dedicated to creating high quality maize-based ingredients that power everyday products across food, nutrition, animal feed, and industrial applications.

    We convert responsibly sourced maize into functional, reliable ingredients that meet the evolving needs of modern manufacturers. Every product we make is guided by a single belief: better ingredients build better outcomes.

    Our Principles

    Strong Values. Strong Ingredients.

    At Saatvik Agro, our values are built on the belief that ingredients matter. What goes into food, feed, and industry must be pure, safe, and responsibly produced.

    We combine ethical sourcing, disciplined processing, and continuous improvement to ensure that every ingredient reflects integrity and care. Our purpose goes beyond manufacturing. We exist to support healthier products, stronger supply chains, and a better future.

    Operations

    Manufactured with Precision. Delivered with Confidence.

    Our manufacturing operations are designed for consistency, hygiene, and efficiency. From maize selection to final dispatch, every stage is monitored to maintain strict quality benchmarks.

    Advanced process controls and quality labs ensure uniformity in texture, moisture, granulation, and performance, enabling manufacturers to operate with confidence and predictability.

    • Fully automated manufacturing unit
    • Dedicated quality assurance systems
    • Scalable production capacity
    Reach

    Built in India with Global Aspirations

    With strategically located manufacturing units and a robust distribution network, Saatvik Agro intends to serve customers across India and International markets.

    Our scale, logistics discipline, and export readiness allow us to support growing businesses with dependable supply and timely delivery.

    Culture

    Powered by People Who Care

    Behind every ingredient is a team committed to excellence.

    At Saatvik Agro, we foster a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous learning. Our people drive innovation, uphold quality, and ensure that every customer receives not just a product, but a partnership built on trust.

    Let's Build Better Ingredients Together

    Whether you are developing new formulations, scaling production, or seeking reliable ingredient partners, Saatvik Agro is ready to support your journey.
