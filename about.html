OUR HISTORY & IDENTITY

About us

Soil was started in 2014 by a team of agronomists and environmentalists who wanted to change how food is grown. Our growth is rooted in a passion for the earth, and we now serve local families, chefs, and schools with organic crops.

By combining traditional organic farming methods with modern sustainable practices, we have transformed over 500 acres of conventional farmland into thriving, chemical-free ecosystems.
